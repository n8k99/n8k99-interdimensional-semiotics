## Reading List
### Round 1
**Semiotics / Meaning Systems:**
- [ ]  _[["Course in General Linguistics"_ — Ferdinand de Saussure]]  #ReadingList
    (Birthplace of semiotics. Understand sign, signifier, signified.)
- [ ] _"Mythologies"_ — Roland Barthes  #ReadingList
    (Symbol systems in culture. How meaning mutates socially.)

**Cognitive Science / Structures of Mind:**
- [ ] _"How to Create a Mind"_ — Ray Kurzweil  #ReadingList
    (Not perfect, but good for thinking about pattern recognition as a basis for meaning.)
- [ ]  _"Women, Fire, and Dangerous Things"_ — George Lakoff  #ReadingList
    (Deep dive into how categorization structures human thought. Big for how meaning could survive transformations.)
    
**Topology / Mathematical Structures:**
- [ ]  _"The Shape of Space"_ — Jeffrey Weeks  #ReadingList
    (Topology explained accessibly — vital if you want to model meaning drift mathematically.)
- [ ] _"Introduction to Topological Manifolds"_ — John M. Lee  #ReadingList
    (More advanced, if you want to be able to _properly_ say "this structure persists under these kinds of transformations.")

**Cosmology / Foundations of Reality:**
- [ ] _"The Fabric of the Cosmos"_ — Brian Greene  #ReadingList
    (How space, time, and causality actually work — and how they might not, under different rules.)
- [ ] _"Many Worlds in One"_ — Alex Vilenkin  #ReadingList
    (Accessible look at multiverse theory; important for thinking about divergent semiotic environments.)

**Speculative Philosophy / Reality and Knowledge:**
- [ ] _"Simulacra and Simulation"_ — Jean Baudrillard  #ReadingList
    (How meaning collapses into itself in layered realities — useful when thinking about semiotic drift across universes.)
- [ ] _"Speculative Realism: An Introduction"_ — Graham Harman  #ReadingList
    (Optional but useful; how to talk about realities beyond human-centered frames.)

---

**If you had to prioritize:**

- Start with Saussure (semiotics) + Greene (cosmology) + Weeks (topology).
- Then layer in Lakoff (cognitive structures) + Vilenkin (multiverse modeling).
- Barthes and Baudrillard are seasoning for understanding symbolic mutation.
