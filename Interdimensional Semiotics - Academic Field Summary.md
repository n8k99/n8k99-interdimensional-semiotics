## The Academic Field: Core Principles

### Definition
Interdimensional Semiotics is the study of meaning survival, symbolic drift, and cognitive persistence across cosmological divergences—investigating how symbols, signs, communication systems, and narrative structures endure or mutate when translated across different dimensions of existence.

### Theoretical Foundations

#### 1. **[[CIF (Cosmic Index Factor)|Cosmic Index Format (CIF)]]**
- **Mathematical Framework**: Uses complex numbers, fractal geometries, and non-Euclidean manifolds to create coordinates resilient to topological discontinuities
- **Multiple Definitions Across Contexts**:
  - **CIF as Multiversal Coefficient**: `CIF = 17π^i` serving as a seed value for divergent worldtracks
  - **Causal Interference Fracture**: Fracture zones where incompatible causal structures overlap
  - **Cultural Infrastructure Framework**: Mapping system for tracking cultural meanings across dimensional frames

#### 2. **[[HEMM Space (Higher-Existence Morphogenic Manifold Space)]]**
- The superstructure containing possible realities and their divergences
- Folding HEMM Space allows for navigation and communication across cosmological boundaries
- Provides the substrate where CIF operations occur

#### 3. **Core Research Areas**
- **Symbolic Persistence Theory**: Which symbols survive transdimensional translation
- **Narrative Drift Models**: How stories mutate when crossing divergent cosmologies
- **Cognitive Anchoring Research**: Mental structures stable across different physical laws
- **Semiotic Gravitational Mass**: Whether meaning possesses narrative inertia across cosmological boundaries

### Saussurian Foundations
The field builds heavily on Ferdinand de Saussure's structural linguistics:
- **Arbitrary Nature of Signs**: Exploring vulnerability of signifier/signified relationships across dimensions
- **Relational Meaning**: How symbolic networks adapt when entering new dimensional contexts
- **Diachronic Analysis**: Whether meaning divergence happens instantly or evolves over time
- **Convention and Social Agreement**: Investigating persistence of semantic conventions without shared history
