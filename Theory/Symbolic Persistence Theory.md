# Symbolic Persistence Theory

> Which symbols survive transdimensional translation, and why.

Studies the differential survival of signs across substrate-transition: which symbols arrive intact, which arrive deformed, and which fail to cross at all. Where [[Semiotic Resilience]] names the property, this subfield studies its distribution — what a surviving symbol has that a lost one lacked. The [[The Tinkerbell Rule|Tinkerbell]] layer is the test bed: an object held in existence by collective honoring either finds new honorers on the far side of a divergence or dissolves into [[Reality is What is Left|the substrate that remains]].

## Role in the field

One of the four subfields declared in the [[Academic Outline]]. Every essay filed to
the field names the subfield it contributes to, so the corpus can be read by line of
inquiry rather than only by date.
