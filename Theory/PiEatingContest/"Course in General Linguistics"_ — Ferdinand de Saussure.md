## Introduction - Chapter 1
Three stages of Linguisitics
1. Grammar - started by the Greeks, continued by the French.  gave rules for correct and incorrect forms
2. Philological - critical tools used to compare texts of different periods, to determine author's voice and deciphering/explaining inscriptions in archaic or obscure languages. Primarily Greek or Latin. Faults are it follows the written langague "too slavishly"
3. Comparative Philology - applied these techniques and skill to comparing langauges. Franz Bopp "To illuminate one language by means of another, to explain the forms of one through the forms of the other"
	1. the discovery of Sanskirt made this possible to compare Greek and Latin. 
	2. Max Muller advanced and popularized comparative philology in _Lessons in the Science of Langaguge_, 1861
**Comparative Philology failed to seek out the nature of its object of study. Obviously, without this elementary step, no science can develop a method.**

...the mistakes of an infant science give a magnified picture of those made by anyone in the first stages of scientific research... .p4
language is no longer looked upon as an organism that develops independently but as a product of the collective mind of linguistic groups.  .p5
