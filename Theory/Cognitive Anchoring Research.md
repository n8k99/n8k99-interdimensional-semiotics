# Cognitive Anchoring Research

> What mental structures remain stable across different physical or temporal laws.

Studies the persistence of mind rather than of message. Where the other subfields follow the sign, this one follows the interpreter: which cognitive structures survive being ported, uploaded, translated, or run on different physics. [[Identity Mutation]] provides the framework and [[Pattern Recognition|PRTM]] the cognitive-substrate basis. The minimum question the [[Academic Outline]] poses: what is the least cognitive apparatus in which meaning can survive interdimensional translation at all?

## Role in the field

One of the four subfields declared in the [[Academic Outline]]. Every essay filed to
the field names the subfield it contributes to, so the corpus can be read by line of
inquiry rather than only by date.
