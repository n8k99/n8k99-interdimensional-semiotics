# Cross-Dimensional Linguistics

> Communication frameworks that survive across universes.

Attempts to model languages, codes, and protocols that remain interpretable across cosmological divergence — the search for what [[Academic Outline|the Outline]] calls semiotic invariants. Takes [[Course_in_General_Linguistics|Saussure]]'s arbitrariness of the sign as its problem statement: if the bond between signifier and signified is arbitrary, nothing guarantees it holds across a substrate change, and any framework that does hold is telling us something non-arbitrary is present.

## Role in the field

One of the four subfields declared in the [[Academic Outline]]. Every essay filed to
the field names the subfield it contributes to, so the corpus can be read by line of
inquiry rather than only by date.
