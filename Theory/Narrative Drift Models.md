# Narrative Drift Models

> How stories, myths, and histories change when crossing between divergent cosmologies.

Models the deformation of narrative under transition. [[Temporal Drift]] covers the within-substrate case (a story changing as its own world moves); [[Symbolic Dissonance]] the between-substrate case (a story arriving somewhere its symbols do not mean the same); [[Narrative Gravity]] the dynamics of high-mass narratives that bend the drift of everything near them. Drift is not corruption — it is the observable signature of a narrative doing the work of surviving.
