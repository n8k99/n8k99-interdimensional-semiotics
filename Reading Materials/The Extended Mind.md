# The Extended Mind

**Authors:** Andy Clark & David Chalmers  
**Year:** 1998  
**Source:** [Original Paper](https://consc.net/papers/extended.html)

## Summary

The seminal paper arguing that cognitive processes can extend beyond the brain into the environment. When external tools (notebooks, phones, AI assistants) play the right functional role in cognition, they become part of the mind itself.

## Key Arguments

- **Parity Principle:** If a process in the world functions as a cognitive process, it *is* a cognitive process
- **Otto and the notebook:** A person with Alzheimer's using a notebook has beliefs stored externally
- **Active externalism:** The mind actively reaches out to incorporate external resources

## Relevance to Thought Police

This paper grounds Topic #8: "Are We Already Cyborgs?" — the idea that our phones, vaults, and AI assistants are already extensions of our cognition. The boundary of "self" is more porous than we assume.
