## Supplement: Distributed Systems and Epistemic Foundations

### Formal Epistemics and Coordination

- ✅ **Knowledge and Common Knowledge in a Distributed Environment** — Joseph Y. Halpern and Yoram Moses (1990)
  - [[Halpern Moses - Knowledge and Common Knowledge in a Distributed Environment|📖 Markdown]]
  - [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0006009) | [PDF](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~manosk/assets/papers/p549-halpern.pdf)
  - *Formal model for reasoning about epistemic knowledge flow in distributed systems. Proves impossibility of [[Common Knowledge|common knowledge]] under unreliable communication. Directly relevant to symbolic persistence across semiotic boundaries.*

### Consensus and Synchrony

- ✅ **Consensus in the Presence of Partial Synchrony** — Cynthia Dwork, Nancy Lynch, and Larry Stockmeyer (1988)
  - [[Dwork Lynch Stockmeyer - Consensus in the Presence of Partial Synchrony|📖 Markdown]]
  - [PDF (MIT CSAIL)](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/tds/papers/Lynch/jacm88.pdf)
  - *Standard escape hatch from FLP impossibility. Upper bounds on message delays make [[Consensus|consensus]] solvable — maps to how semiotic systems stabilize meaning under partial observability. 2007 Dijkstra Prize.*

### Impossibility Results for Distributed Guarantees

- ✅ **Brewer's Conjecture and the Feasibility of Consistent, Available, Partition-Tolerant Web Services** — Seth Gilbert and Nancy Lynch (2002)
  - [[Gilbert Lynch - Brewers Conjecture and the CAP Theorem|📖 Markdown]]
  - [PDF (CMU)](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~adrian/731-sp04/readings/GL-cap.pdf)
  - *The [[CAP Theorem]]: any distributed system must choose at most two of Consistency, Availability, and [[Partition Tolerance|Partition tolerance]]. Models the fundamental tradeoffs in cross-dimensional meaning preservation.*
