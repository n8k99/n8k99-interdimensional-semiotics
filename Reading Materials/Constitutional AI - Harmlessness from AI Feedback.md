# Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback

**Source:** Anthropic Research  
**URL:** [Anthropic Paper](https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback)

## Summary

Anthropic's approach to AI alignment through a "constitution" — a set of principles the AI uses to critique and revise its own outputs. Rather than relying solely on human feedback, the AI learns to self-correct based on explicit values.

## Key Concepts

- **Constitutional Principles:** Written rules the AI uses to evaluate its responses
- **Self-Critique:** AI generates responses, then critiques them against the constitution
- **RLHF Alternative:** Reduces dependence on human labelers for safety training
- **Explicit Values:** The AI knows what it values and why
