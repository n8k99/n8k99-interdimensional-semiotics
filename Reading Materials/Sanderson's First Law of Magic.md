# Sanderson's First Law of Magic

**Author:** Brandon Sanderson  
**URL:** [Sanderson's Blog](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/sandersons-first-law/)

## The Law

> "An author's ability to solve problems with magic is directly proportional to how well the reader understands said magic."

## Summary

Sanderson's framework for designing magic systems in fantasy. The more a magic system is used to resolve plot problems, the more it needs clear rules. "Soft" magic (mysterious, unexplained) works for wonder; "hard" magic (systematic, rule-based) works for problem-solving.

## Key Concepts

- **Hard vs Soft Magic:** Systematic rules vs. mysterious wonder
- **Reader Understanding:** Rules must be clear if magic solves problems
- **Limitations Matter:** What magic *can't* do is as important as what it can
- **Cost and Consequence:** Magic should have trade-offs
