# YAGNI

> You Aren't Gonna Need It.

**Attribution:** Ron Jeffries (Extreme Programming)
**Category:** Design

## Statement

Do not implement functionality until it is actually required. Speculative generality is a cost — code, tests, documentation, cognitive load — paid now against a benefit that may never materialize. Defer until demand is real.
