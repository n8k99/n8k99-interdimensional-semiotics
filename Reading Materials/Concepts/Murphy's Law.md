# Murphy's Law

> Anything that can go wrong will go wrong.

**Attribution:** Edward A. Murphy Jr., 1949
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

Not mysticism — a design principle. If a failure mode is physically possible, assume it will occur under load. Systems designed to Murphy's Law have safety margins, redundancy, and graceful degradation; systems that aren't, don't.
