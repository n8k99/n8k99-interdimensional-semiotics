# Amara's Law

> We tend to overestimate the effect of a technology in the short run and underestimate the effect in the long run.

**Attribution:** Roy Amara
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

The Gartner Hype Cycle is the operational form. Early excitement exceeds near-term delivery; disillusionment follows; eventual impact — arriving slowly — exceeds what even the hype promised.
