# Hanlon's Razor

> Never attribute to malice that which is adequately explained by stupidity.

**Attribution:** Robert J. Hanlon
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

Or, less harshly: by error, by miscommunication, by incentive misalignment. Malicious explanations are expensive — they demand agency, planning, and motive. Boring explanations usually suffice and should be tried first.
