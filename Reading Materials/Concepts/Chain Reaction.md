# Chain Reaction

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Multiplication]] into rigorous physics and chemistry.

A **chain reaction** is a self-propagating sequence in which each event produces the conditions for further events, so the process amplifies itself across a population. In a **nuclear chain reaction**, each fission releases neutrons that trigger further fissions; in chemical chain reactions, radicals regenerate radicals. The controlling quantity is the **multiplication factor** *k* — the average number of follow-on events each event produces. At *k* < 1 the chain dies out; at *k* = 1 it is self-sustaining (critical); at *k* > 1 it grows exponentially (supercritical). Where alchemy spoke of *multiplication* — re-circulating the elixir so each cycle augments its potency and reach — physics gives the precise control parameter: the multiplication factor that separates a dying process from a self-sustaining, amplifying one.

## Why criticality is a Branch Point

The criticality threshold *k* = 1 is a sharp **branch point**. On one side the system dissipates; on the other it amplifies without bound. The same configuration, nudged across the critical multiplication factor, lands on radically different sheets — quiescent or runaway — and the crossing is a [[Phase Transition]] (the percolation of a self-sustaining cluster). At criticality the system sits *at* the [[Branch Point]], and an infinitesimal change in *k* commits it to one sheet. Beyond it, the amplification is an [[Emergence|emergent]] macroscopic behaviour absent from any single event — the whole becomes qualitatively different from the sum of its triggers.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Chain reaction is the rigorous hinge for [[Multiplication]] (operation 11): *to multiply is to drive a chain reaction supercritical*. In the sheet-crossing reading it is the **bridge-replication stage** — the dynamics by which a *single* successful [[Branch Point]] crossing is amplified into a repeatable, scalable [[Bridge Activation]]. Raising *k* past 1 is exactly converting a hard, one-off crossing into a self-sustaining, multiplying channel between [[Sheet|sheets]]: each traversal lowers the cost of the next, widening the channel until crossing is routine rather than exceptional. Multiplication converts a *characterized* branch point into an *operationalized* one — worth registering in the [[CIF (Cosmic Index Factor)]].
