# Parkinson's Law

> Work expands to fill the time available for its completion.

**Attribution:** C. Northcote Parkinson, 1955
**Category:** Planning

## Statement

Give a task a week and it takes a week. Give it a day and it takes a day — often at comparable quality. Deadlines do not merely *measure* work; they *shape* it. Scope quietly inflates to match whatever budget is announced.
