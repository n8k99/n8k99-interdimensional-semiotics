# Goodhart's Law

> When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.

**Attribution:** Charles Goodhart, 1975 (Marilyn Strathern's phrasing, 1997)
**Category:** Planning

## Statement

Measurement changes the thing measured. The moment a proxy is used for reward or punishment, humans optimize the proxy, and the correlation between proxy and goal decays. Every KPI in history has eventually demonstrated this.
