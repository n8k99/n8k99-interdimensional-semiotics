# Nucleation

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Projection]] (and the [[Precipitation]] process) into rigorous physics.

**Nucleation** is the initiating event of a phase transition: the formation of the first stable seed of a new phase within a metastable parent phase. A supercooled liquid, a supersaturated solution, a superheated vapour — each can persist in its metastable state until a **critical nucleus** appears, a seed just large enough that growth lowers the system's energy rather than raising it. Below the critical size, fluctuating clusters dissolve back; at and above it, the seed grows and the new phase sweeps through the whole. In the classic alchemical image of *projection*, a single grain of the perfected Stone cast onto a mass of molten metal transmutes the whole at once — which is nucleation exactly: a tiny seed triggering the wholesale conversion of a metastable bulk.

## Why nucleation IS a Branch Point

Nucleation is among the cleanest physical realizations of a **branch point**. The metastable bulk sits poised at a singular locus — it *can* transition, but local information does not determine *when, where, or into which* new-phase sheet. The nucleation event is the navigational act: the appearance of the critical seed selects a site, an orientation, a polymorph (which crystal sheet), and commits the whole bulk to it. Identical metastable systems nucleate at different sites and land on different sheets; the nucleus is the [[Branch Point]] that joins the metastable sheet to the new-phase sheet, and the choice made there is irreversible [[Symmetry Breaking]]. It is the trigger of the [[Phase Transition]] — the zero-measure event of infinite significance, on which the whole transformation hinges.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Nucleation is the rigorous hinge for [[Projection]] (operation 12, the culminating cast of the Stone) and the abrupt-threshold [[Precipitation]] process: *to project is to nucleate a transmutation*. In the sheet-crossing reading it is the **completed [[Branch Point]] crossing triggered by a seed** — the instant the perfected agent is cast and the base material crosses the branch point *all at once*, the [[Substrate Materialization]] of a new body on the destination [[Sheet]]. The [[Universal Seed]] is the alchemical name for the critical nucleus: the minimal kernel from which a full materialization unfolds under iteration. Projection is monodromy applied at scale and completion — and nucleation is the physics that makes a single grain transmute a mass.
