# Narrative Gravity

> **Stub.** Framework-seed named in the original April 2025 IntSem repo and brought into the vault to be developed.

## Working Definition

**Narrative Gravity** names the framework for how narratives accrete weight and bend the interpretive space around them. Once a narrative reaches sufficient mass — measured in honoring, in retellings, in citations, in cultural saturation — *other narratives are pulled toward it, deformed in passing, captured in its orbit*. New narratives that conflict with high-mass narratives have to either match or exceed the gravity to displace them, or accept being read in light of the high-mass narrative whether or not the new narrative wants that.

## Why It Matters in the Field

This is the IntSem-relevant generalization of [[Hub|hub]]-effects in [[Scale-free Network|scale-free networks]] applied to narrative-and-meaning rather than to nodes-and-edges. High-mass narratives (the Christian salvation arc; the Hero's Journey; the Marxist class-struggle narrative; the AI-singularity narrative) bend interpretation around them by their mere presence. New texts that *would* be readable on their own terms get re-read through the high-mass narrative's frame because the gravity is too great to escape.

This connects to [[Semiotic Mass]] — the related concept already in the vault — and probably the two should be developed in parallel or merged into a single framework.

## Open Development

The framework needs:
- Mathematical formalization — possibly via field-theory analogies (narratives as mass-bearing objects in interpretive space) or via network-theory hub-dynamics
- Worked examples — historical cases where high-mass narratives captured smaller ones (every gospel narrative read through the Christ pattern; every revolutionary movement read through 1789 or 1917; every AI capability evaluated against AGI-narrative)
- Distinction from [[Semiotic Mass]] (existing vault concept) — clarify whether they're the same thing under different names, or complementary
- Connection to [[Preferential Attachment]] — high-mass narratives attract more honoring, which makes them more massive, in a positive feedback loop
- Application to AI-discourse — how the few high-mass narratives (Skynet, Singularity, Stochastic Parrots) bend smaller technical discussions around them
