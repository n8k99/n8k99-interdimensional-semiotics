# ⧗ Wick

## Definition

The **Wick** is the partial order running through the nested-matrix structure of [[HEMM Space]] — the axis of abstraction connecting cosmi at different depths of the recursive state representation. A cosmos that is **upwick** of another is more mathematically fundamental, less materially contingent, closer to structural invariants. A cosmos that is **downwick** is more concrete, more specifically instantiated, more dependent on particular physical constants.

The Wick is not a ladder. It is a directed acyclic graph — a **web**. Two cosmi can be mutually wick-lateral (neither upwick nor downwick of each other), connected only through shared ancestors, shared descendants, or not at all. There is no global top, no global bottom, and no guarantee that a path between two given cosmi exists in either direction.

In Calculator terminology, the same concept is called the **Invariant Ground**: "closer to the Ground" means "closer to the mathematics that does not depend on any specific cosmos to be true."

## Properties

The wick relation $\preceq$ satisfies:
- **Reflexivity:** every cosmos is upwick of itself ($A \preceq A$)
- **Antisymmetry:** if $A \preceq B$ and $B \preceq A$, then $A = B$
- **Transitivity:** if $A \preceq B$ and $B \preceq C$, then $A \preceq C$

And, critically:
- **Not totality.** For many pairs $A, B$: $A \npreceq B$ and $B \npreceq A$.

This non-totality is the defining feature. The Wick is not a hierarchy with clean "above" and "below." Navigation along the Wick requires specifying a **wick-path** — a sequence of ordered steps — because the destination's relative position to the origin is only defined along paths that exist in the partial order.

A complete address in the Cosmic Index Format must include not just the nesting depth but the wick-path by which that depth was reached. Two Level-$N$ addresses with identical nested matrices but different wick-paths describe entirely different manifold sectors.

## Application Across Domains

### Cosmological

Orbis may sit simultaneously downwick of three more-abstract cosmi and upwick of seven more-contingent ones, while having no wick-relationship with thousands of other sheets. The Elemental Planes are reachable from Orbis via branch points that trace specific wick-paths — not "above" or "below," but laterally accessible through the web. ⟦ [[All Possible Books|All Potential Books]] ⟧ is an upwick chamber: its contents are not authored but entailed by mathematics at a higher nesting level.

### Semiotic

Genre sits upwick of instance. The *concept* of the sonnet sits upwick of any particular sonnet. A style — Bauhaus, Swiss Design, Memphis — sits upwick of every object made in that style. Upwick structures have fewer contingent parameters; downwick structures are richer in specificity. The Wick formalises a relationship that literary critics, designers, and philosophers have always named in a hundred other ways: *form vs. instance*, *type vs. token*, *archetype vs. example*, *theory vs. implementation*.

### Computational

Surfaces in the [[Noosphere Ghosts]] operating model live at different wick-depths by design. Claudian-in-Obsidian and Claude Code at repository root sit upwick — they see schema, project graph, invariants. [[ChatHUD]], tasks-chat, Discord sit downwick — they see specific turns, specific keybinds, specific moments. Attempting to flatten the Ghost's surfaces into equivalent "smart" is a category error: each surface should operate at its own wick-depth, connected through a shared [[Branch Point|branch point]] (the nexus). The resolver protocol of [[InnateScript]] is, in effect, a language for specifying wick-paths for commissions: "resolve this reference at *this* level of abstraction, then bind it at *that* level of instantiation."

## Why It Is Called the Wick

A candle's wick conveys fuel upward and flame downward — the same physical axis serves both directions of flow, and the flame exists only where the two meet. The metaphor does more work than it first appears: the Wick is an axis on which abstraction flows one way and instantiation flows the other, and structure ignites only where the two currents intersect. A cosmos without wick-relationships in either direction has neither ancestors to receive form from nor descendants to give form to — it is a candle with no wick.
