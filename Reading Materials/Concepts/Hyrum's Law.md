# Hyrum's Law

> With a sufficient number of users of an API, it does not matter what you promise in the contract: all observable behaviors of your system will be depended on by somebody.

**Attribution:** Hyrum Wright (Google)
**Category:** Architecture

## Statement

Undocumented behavior becomes documented behavior through usage. Bug-for-bug compatibility is a real engineering requirement. The surface area of a released system is not its interface — it's everything externally observable.
