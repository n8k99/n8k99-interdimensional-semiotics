# Putt's Law

> Technology is dominated by two types of people: those who understand what they do not manage, and those who manage what they do not understand.

**Attribution:** Archibald Putt, *Putt's Law and the Successful Technocrat*
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

A corollary of the Peter Principle specific to technical hierarchies. Creates a structural gap between technical judgment and decision authority that organizations spend enormous energy trying — usually badly — to close.
