# Map Is Not the Territory

> A representation of a thing is not the thing itself.

**Attribution:** Alfred Korzybski, 1931
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

Models, diagrams, and abstractions are useful precisely because they omit detail — but every omission is a place where reality can surprise you. The map is only as good as the fidelity of its omissions.
