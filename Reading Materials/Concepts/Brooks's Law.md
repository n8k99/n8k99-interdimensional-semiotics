# Brooks's Law

> Adding people to a late project makes it later.

**Attribution:** Fred Brooks, *The Mythical Man-Month*, 1975
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

New team members require onboarding, and coordination overhead grows combinatorially with headcount. In the short term the cost of integrating additional labour outweighs the labour added. Months are not fungible with bodies.
