# Autocatalysis

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Fermentation]] into rigorous chemistry and biology.

**Autocatalysis** is catalysis in which a product of the reaction is also a catalyst *for that same reaction* — so the reaction accelerates itself: the more product, the faster it is made. An **autocatalytic set** is a network of molecules that collectively catalyses its own reproduction, the candidate chemistry of the origin of life. **Microbial metabolism** is the living instance: a ferment (yeast, bacteria) introduced into a medium reproduces itself while transforming the whole substrate in its own image. Where alchemy spoke of *fermentation* — a living leaven sown into dead matter, propagating a new organizing life through it — biology gives the mechanism: an autocatalytic, self-reproducing pattern that recruits the surrounding matter into its own order.

## Why this is a Branch Point seeded from one side

Autocatalysis is a **branch point with positive feedback**. An autocatalytic system has a bistable structure: below a threshold concentration of the catalyst-product, the reaction stalls; above it, the self-amplification runs away and reorganizes the whole medium. The seed-introduction tips the system across that [[Branch Point]] — and crucially, the crossing is *initiated from the destination side*: a fragment of the target state (the ferment) is planted, and the target's organizing pattern propagates back across the branch point to claim the substrate before it has fully crossed. The new order is an [[Emergence|emergent]], [[Self-Organizing Systems|self-organizing]] structure that the small seed could not have specified atom-by-atom — it arises from the autocatalytic dynamics.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Autocatalysis is the rigorous hinge for [[Fermentation]] (operation 10): *to ferment is to seed an autocatalytic process*. In the sheet-crossing reading it is the **seeding stage** — a small pattern from the *destination* [[Sheet]] introduced into the prima materia, whose order then self-amplifies through the whole. It is a [[Bridge Activation]] initiated from the far side: the bridge activated by a self-reproducing seed rather than by external force. The new life that fermentation sows is precisely an autocatalytic set taking hold in dead matter and raising it — and what later [[Multiplication]] ([[Chain Reaction]]) amplifies.
