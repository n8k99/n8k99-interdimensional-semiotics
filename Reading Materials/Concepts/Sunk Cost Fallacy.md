# Sunk Cost Fallacy

> The tendency to continue investing in something because of already-incurred, unrecoverable costs.

**Attribution:** Behavioral economics
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

Rational actors consider only future costs and future benefits. Real actors feel the weight of what's already spent. This produces continued investment in failing projects, features, and relationships long past the point where abandonment would be correct.
