# Testing Pyramid

> Have many fast unit tests, fewer integration tests, and very few end-to-end tests.

**Attribution:** Mike Cohn, 2009
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

The shape reflects cost-per-test and failure-diagnosis cost. Inverted pyramids — heavy on E2E, light on unit — are expensive to run, flaky, and slow to diagnose when they fail.
