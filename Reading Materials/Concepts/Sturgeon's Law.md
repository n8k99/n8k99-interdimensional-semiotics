# Sturgeon's Law

> Ninety percent of everything is crap.

**Attribution:** Theodore Sturgeon, 1957
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

Originally a defense of science fiction against critics who only read its worst examples. Applies to code reviews, library ecosystems, and engineering output in general. The interesting 10% is what's worth attention.
