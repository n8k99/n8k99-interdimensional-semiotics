# Cunningham's Law

> The best way to get the right answer on the internet is not to ask a question; it's to post the wrong answer.

**Attribution:** Ward Cunningham (apocryphal)
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

People who will not answer a question will happily correct a mistake. A description of online social dynamics, but also — uncomfortably — a working tactic.
