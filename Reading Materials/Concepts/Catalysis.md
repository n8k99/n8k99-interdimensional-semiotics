# Catalysis

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Digestion]] and the [[Philosopher's Stone]] into rigorous chemistry and biology.

**Catalysis** is the acceleration of a chemical reaction by a **catalyst** — a species that lowers the reaction's activation barrier by providing an alternative pathway, and which *emerges from the reaction unconsumed*, free to act again. **Enzymes** are biological catalysts: proteins that perform exactly this office at body temperature, the molecular machinery of digestion, metabolism, and life. Where alchemy spoke of *digestion* — gentle sustained heat ripening a substance over time so its hidden virtues are drawn out — modern biochemistry sees enzymatic catalysis: a catalyst holding a substrate in a reactive conformation and lowering the barrier to its transformation, sustained over the duration the reaction requires.

## The catalyst as a held-open Branch Point

A catalyst's defining trick is that it *changes which reaction pathways are accessible* without being consumed — it lowers one barrier among many, biasing the system toward one product-sheet. This is branch-point engineering: the catalyst *reshapes the local manifold* so that a particular [[Branch Point]] crossing becomes accessible that was kinetically forbidden before, while the catalyst itself, passing through unchanged, is the agent that *keeps the crossing-channel open*. It is therefore the chemical face of [[Bridge Activation]]: the operator that makes a sheet-crossing happen and survives it. The catalytic cycle — bind, transform, release, repeat — is a small [[Self-Organizing Systems|self-organizing]] loop that sustains a flux across the barrier rather than performing a single discrete jump.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Catalysis is the rigorous hinge for [[Digestion]] (operation 5, sustained gentle transformation) and for the [[Philosopher's Stone]] itself — *the* catalyst, "the operator upon materials, the catalyst whose presence effects the change while passing through it unconsumed." In the sheet-crossing reading, catalysis is the **incubation of a [[Bridge Activation]]**: the agent that holds the crossing-channel open and warm over the maturation period, lowering the calibration cost of the [[Branch Point]] crossing without being spent. The Stone's "pass through unconsumed" is the catalyst's defining property stated in alchemical language; [[Putrefaction]]'s decay is enzymatic catalysis run as breakdown.
