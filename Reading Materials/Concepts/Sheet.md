# ≋ Sheet

## Definition

A **sheet** is a maximal connected region of a multi-sheeted manifold on which a given multi-valued function of existence resolves to a single value. In a [[Riemann Surface|Riemann surface]], each sheet is one single-valued branch of an otherwise ambiguous function, glued to the other sheets at [[Branch Point|branch points]] where the function's multi-valuedness is localised.

In [[Interdimensional Semiotics]] — and specifically in [[HEMM Space]] — a sheet is a **plane of existence**: a locally self-consistent cosmos, plane, worldtrack, or domain of signification in which one set of physical laws, semiotic rules, or interpretive conventions hold uniformly. Different sheets can share identical manifold addresses and still be causally disjoint, because their orientation on the underlying state space differs.

A sheet is not a place. It is a *consistency zone* — the largest patch on which things hold together.

## Properties

- **Local Euclideanness.** Within a sheet, ordinary coordinate systems and ordinary physics apply. Inhabitants of a single sheet need not be aware that other sheets exist — their sheet is internally complete.
- **Boundary via [[Branch Point|branch points]].** Sheets are not walled off. They are joined at discrete points (or cuts) at which a traveller crosses from one sheet to the next. The boundary is zero-measure; most of the sheet has no contact with its neighbours.
- **Sheet-disjointness at shared address.** Two sheets may be "at the same place" as measured in the coordinate column of the state matrix and still be mutually invisible, because their [[Monodromy|monodromy]] / spin orientation differs.
- **Non-total ordering.** Sheets do not stack in a line. Some sheets are [[Wick|upwick]] of others, some are downwick, and vast regions are wick-lateral — not ordered against each other at all.

## Application Across Domains

### Cosmological

In [[HEMM Space]], each causal sphere — a universe, a plane, a cosmos — is a sheet of the manifold. The [[Pentagonal Harmonic]] is the configuration of five [[Branch Point|branch points]] on Orbis's sheet where it touches adjacent elemental-plane sheets. Sheets are the unit in which "a reality" is defined, and the manifold is the stitching that makes the plural of reality meaningful.

### Semiotic

A meaning-sheet is the maximal zone in which one sign-system is univocal. Within a single culture or idiolect, a polysemous word has one operative reading per sheet; the word's full semantic space is multi-sheeted and connected at branch points of contextual overlap. Translation between languages, between registers, between specialist discourses is sheet-crossing — and what survives the crossing is what the [[Topological Invariant|topological invariants]] of the semantic manifold preserve.

### Computational

In the [[Noosphere Ghosts]] operating model, each conversational surface — Claude Code, Claudian, ChatHUD, tasks-chat, ChatGPT, Discord — is a sheet. Each surface has its own base physics: context window, tool access, UI affordances, memory horizon. A persona crossing surfaces is crossing sheets. What persists across the crossing is everything with enough [[Semiotic Mass|semiotic mass]] to survive the [[Monodromy|monodromy]] transformation; what does not persist is everything whose stability was sheet-local.
