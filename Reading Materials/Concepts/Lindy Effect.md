# Lindy Effect

> The future life expectancy of a non-perishable thing is proportional to its current age.

**Attribution:** Albert Goldman / Nassim Taleb
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

Technologies, ideas, and institutions that have survived a long time are more likely to survive further. Vim, SQL, and C have high Lindy lifespans. This year's framework does not — yet.
