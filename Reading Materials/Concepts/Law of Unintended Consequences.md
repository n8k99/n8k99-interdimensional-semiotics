# Law of Unintended Consequences

> Actions of people, and especially of governments and complex systems, have effects that are unanticipated or unintended.

**Attribution:** Robert K. Merton, 1936
**Category:** Architecture

## Statement

In software: every change to a complex system produces side effects the change author did not predict. The size of the side-effect tail grows with the coupling of the system. Rollback plans exist because this law exists.
