# KISS

> Keep It Simple, Stupid.

**Attribution:** Kelly Johnson, Lockheed Skunk Works
**Category:** Design

## Statement

Designs should be as simple as possible and no simpler. A working simple system is preferable to a theoretically superior complex one, because the complex one's behavior cannot be reasoned about under pressure.
