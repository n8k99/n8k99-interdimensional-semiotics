# Linus's Law

> Given enough eyeballs, all bugs are shallow.

**Attribution:** Linus Torvalds (via Eric S. Raymond, 1999)
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

Open-source theorem: scale of review turns deep bugs into findable ones. Contested empirically — many eyes do not actually look — but the underlying principle (visibility enables correction) is sound where visibility is real.
