# Broken Windows Theory

> Visible disorder and disrepair invite further disorder and disrepair.

**Attribution:** Wilson and Kelling, 1982 (software application, Pragmatic Programmer)
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

In code: one hacky workaround signals that hacks are acceptable here, which invites more. In urban policy the theory is contested; in codebases its operational form — fix small problems now or watch them multiply — is broadly observed.
