# Bulshytt

**Bulshytt** is language with the posture of meaning and none of the load: utterance shaped to pass as explanation, argument, feeling, or vision while refusing contact with the substrate it claims to describe.

The term comes through [[Anathem]] and is useful to IntSem because it separates a linguistic failure from a technological one. Bulshytt is older than AI. AI merely industrializes its production.

## Definition

Bulshytt is present when:

- the sentence performs the social shape of thought;
- no risk is taken against [[Reality is What is Left]];
- disagreement cannot find a claim solid enough to test;
- the words preserve status, momentum, or mood while avoiding substrate contact;
- the listener is pressured to honor the utterance as meaningful before meaning has been earned.

Bulshytt differs from error. Error can be corrected because it has touched something. Bulshytt avoids touch.

## Relation to Artificial Inanity

[[Artificial Inanity]] is a high-volume contemporary ecology in which Bulshytt becomes cheap to produce and expensive to filter. Bulshytt is the local linguistic object; Artificial Inanity is the production and distribution condition that makes such objects ambient.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Bulshytt is a Tinkerbell pollutant. It asks a cohort to honor a sign as meaningful while leaving no residue when the honoring is subtracted. Enough Bulshytt in a basin causes [[Narrative Drift]] toward genericity because participants lose the habit of asking what the words actually carry.
