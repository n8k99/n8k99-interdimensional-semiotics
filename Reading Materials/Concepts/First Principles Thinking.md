# First Principles Thinking

> Decompose a problem into its fundamental truths and reason upward from there.

**Attribution:** Aristotle; repopularized by Elon Musk
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

The alternative — reasoning by analogy — inherits the constraints of whatever was analogized. First-principles reasoning is expensive but occasionally produces moves that analogical reasoning cannot see.
