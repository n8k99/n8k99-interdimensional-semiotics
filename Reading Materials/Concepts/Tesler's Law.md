# Tesler's Law

> Every application has an inherent amount of irreducible complexity. The only question is: who will have to deal with it?

**Attribution:** Larry Tesler
**Category:** Architecture

## Statement

Also called the Law of Conservation of Complexity. You can push complexity onto the user, onto the developer, or onto the system — but you cannot make it vanish. Good design is a deliberate choice about *who pays the cost*.
