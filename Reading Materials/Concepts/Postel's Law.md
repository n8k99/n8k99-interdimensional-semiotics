# Postel's Law

> Be conservative in what you send, be liberal in what you accept.

**Attribution:** Jon Postel, 1980 (RFC 761)
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

Also called the Robustness Principle. Produces interoperability at scale. Controversial in modern security thinking — liberal acceptance can mean accepting malformed input that later becomes an attack surface — but still foundational to the internet's working.
