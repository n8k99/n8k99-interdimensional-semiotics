# Gilb's Law

> Anything you need to quantify can be measured in some way that is superior to not measuring it at all.

**Attribution:** Tom Gilb
**Category:** Planning

## Statement

A counterweight to Goodhart: imperfect measurement is still better than no measurement. The trick is to measure with humility — treating the number as a signal, not a contract.
