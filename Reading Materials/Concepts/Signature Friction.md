# Signature Friction

**Signature Friction** is the irreducible roughness by which a meaning-bearing artifact, practice, or narrative proves the live substrate that produced it.

It is not all roughness. A typo from broken hardware, a stale ritual, a confusing document, or a bad workflow can be ordinary drag. Signature Friction is the subset of resistance that carries authorship, body-pressure, local intelligence, cohort memory, tool-history, contradiction, or world-specific bearing. Remove it and the artifact may become smoother while becoming less anchored.

## Function

Signature Friction constrains [[Narrative Drift]]. When a story crosses substrates, the receiving substrate may preserve the story's surface elements while shaving away the pressures that made those elements meaningful. The result is genericity: the artifact still resembles the source but no longer carries its load.

The receiving substrate does not have to preserve the same surface roughness. It must preserve, translate, or honestly replace the pressure the roughness carried.

## Distinction from Friction-Shave

[[Friction-Shave]] removes resistance that obstructs live motion. Signature Friction names resistance that signs the work.

The practical test:

> Is this friction obstructing the live motion, or is this friction signing the live substrate?

The test is not always answerable immediately. Some roughness must remain under pressure long enough to reveal whether it is dirt or ore.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Signature Friction gives [[Narrative Drift Models]] a preservation criterion beyond semiotic mass and resilience. A narrative remains itself across substrate transition when the receiving substrate preserves the load carried by its signature frictions. A narrative drifts toward genericity when those frictions are treated as ordinary drag and shaved away.

The concept is adjacent to [[Low-Background Steel]] and [[Substrate-Honest Genealogy]]: all three ask what survives polish, contamination, and official framing as evidence of the real conditions of production.
