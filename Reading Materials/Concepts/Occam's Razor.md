# Occam's Razor

> Entities should not be multiplied without necessity — the simplest explanation is usually correct.

**Attribution:** William of Ockham, 14th c.
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

A heuristic, not a theorem. Among competing hypotheses, prefer the one with fewer assumptions, because each assumption is an opportunity to be wrong. Not a guarantee of truth, but a good default.
