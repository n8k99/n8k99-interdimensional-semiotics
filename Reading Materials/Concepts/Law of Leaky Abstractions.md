# Law of Leaky Abstractions

> All non-trivial abstractions, to some degree, are leaky.

**Attribution:** Joel Spolsky, 2002
**Category:** Architecture

## Statement

No abstraction fully hides what it abstracts over. TCP promises a reliable stream but leaks latency; an ORM promises objects but leaks SQL semantics. Consequence: you cannot use a deep stack without eventually understanding the layers beneath.
