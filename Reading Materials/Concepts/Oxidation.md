# Oxidation

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Calcination]] (and the [[Spirit of Fire]]) into rigorous chemistry, so the alchemy→IntSem bridge builds out its theory as it crosses.

**Oxidation** is the loss of electrons by a chemical species — formally one half of a coupled *redox* (reduction-oxidation) reaction in which electrons transfer from one reactant to another. **Combustion** is rapid, self-sustaining, exothermic oxidation: a fuel reacts with an oxidiser (typically O₂) fast enough that the released energy maintains the reaction. Where pre-modern alchemy saw *calcination* — fire reducing a body to incombustible ash — modern chemistry sees oxidation: the combustible fraction's bonds are broken and its atoms recombine with oxygen, releasing energy and leaving the oxidised mineral residue (the *calx*). Oxidation is the science behind burning, rusting, respiration, and the slow tarnish of metals: a single mechanism spanning the explosive and the imperceptible.

## Why this is a Branch Point

Combustion is a textbook **branch point**. Below its ignition temperature a fuel-oxidiser mixture is metastable — it sits on the unburnt sheet indefinitely. At the ignition threshold the system crosses a critical point and is committed, irreversibly, to the combusted sheet: the reaction runs to completion and *cannot be analytically continued back* to the unburnt state along the same path. The pre-combustion configuration is a single locus from which multiple product-sheets are reachable (complete combustion to CO₂ + H₂O, incomplete combustion to CO + soot, the various calcination residues), and which sheet the system lands on is determined by conditions at the threshold, not by the fuel alone. This is the [[Branch Point]] structure exactly: local under-determination resolved by a navigational event at a singular point. The ignition point is the branch point; the product distributions are the sheets; the combustion event is the monodromy that lands the system on one of them, transformed rather than merely relocated.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Oxidation supplies the rigorous hinge for [[Calcination]], the first alchemical operation: *to calcine is to oxidise*. In the sheet-crossing reading, oxidation is the **ignition of a [[Branch Point]] crossing** — the chemistry by which a substrate's combustible binding to its current [[Sheet]] is broken, leaving the oxidised residue as the navigable carrier that the later operations transport. It is simultaneously a [[Phase Transition]] (the ignition threshold is a critical point) and a [[Symmetry Breaking]] (the symmetric metastable mixture commits to one product-sheet). The [[Sulfur (Principle)|Sulfur]] principle — combustibility, the soul that burns — is the alchemical name for *oxidisability*: how readily a substrate's identity-charge is consumed in the crossing.
