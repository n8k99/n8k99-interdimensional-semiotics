# Inversion

> Solve problems backward: instead of asking how to succeed, ask how to guarantee failure, then avoid those paths.

**Attribution:** Carl Jacobi; Charlie Munger
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

"Invert, always invert." Often easier to identify what makes a system fail than what makes it succeed. The negative space of a design is frequently clearer than the positive.
