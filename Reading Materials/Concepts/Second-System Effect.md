# Second-System Effect

> The second system an architect designs is the most dangerous they will ever design.

**Attribution:** Fred Brooks, *The Mythical Man-Month*
**Category:** Architecture

## Statement

Having learned restraint on their first system, they now over-generalize, over-feature, and over-engineer their second — trying to include every idea they had to cut the first time. The successful replacement becomes the bloated replacement.
