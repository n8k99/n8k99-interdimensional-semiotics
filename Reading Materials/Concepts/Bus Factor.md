# Bus Factor

> The minimum number of team members whose sudden loss would halt the project.

**Attribution:** Software folklore
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

A bus factor of 1 means one person's absence breaks the system. Low bus factor is a form of concentrated risk masquerading as expertise. Documentation, pairing, and rotation are the standard mitigations.
