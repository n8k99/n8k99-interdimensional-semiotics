# Lehman's Laws of Software Evolution

> Software systems used in the real world must continually change or become progressively less useful; as they change, their structural complexity increases unless work is done to reduce it.

**Attribution:** Meir M. Lehman, 1974–1996
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

Eight laws in total, but the core two are Continuing Change and Increasing Complexity. Software is not built and finished — it is cultivated or abandoned.
