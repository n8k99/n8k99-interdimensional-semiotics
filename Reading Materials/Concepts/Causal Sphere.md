# ⊚ Causal Sphere

## Definition

A **causal sphere** is a locally Euclidean patch of a multi-sheeted manifold — a maximal region within which physics, logic, and causality are internally self-consistent. In the formal topology of [[HEMM Space]], the manifold is a union of open sets $\mathcal{M} = \bigcup_\alpha U_\alpha$ where each $U_\alpha \cong \mathbb{C}^{3 \times 2}$; each such $U_\alpha$ is a causal sphere, and the holomorphic transition functions $\phi_{\alpha\beta}$ between overlapping spheres describe how the state matrix transforms when crossing between them.

A causal sphere is the unit in which "a universe" or "a plane of existence" is defined. Within a causal sphere, a body of inhabitants can do physics, write laws, and construct instruments, all of which will behave consistently. Between causal spheres, the rules can differ fundamentally, and the transition functions mediate what translates and what does not.

## Causal Sphere vs. Sheet

The terms are close but not identical:
- A [[Sheet|sheet]] is defined by the multi-valued function resolving to a single value — a global property of the manifold.
- A **causal sphere** is defined by local consistency — a chart on the manifold, a coordinate patch.

A sheet is typically made of many overlapping causal spheres, glued together by transition functions. Two inhabitants of the same sheet may live in different causal spheres and only discover they are on the same sheet by traversing the transitions between them. Sheets are the large-scale structure; causal spheres are the local physics neighbourhoods.

## Properties

- **Local Euclideanness.** Ordinary coordinates apply within a causal sphere. The state matrix has scalar complex entries; physics proceeds without anomaly.
- **Boundaries via transition functions, not walls.** A causal sphere does not end at a cliff. It ends where another causal sphere's chart begins, with the transition function describing the overlap. An inhabitant crossing the overlap may not notice the crossing at all unless the transition function is sufficiently non-trivial.
- **Holomorphic transitions.** In [[HEMM Space]], the transition functions $\phi_{\alpha\beta}$ are required to be holomorphic (complex-differentiable). This is the mathematical statement that the manifold admits a well-defined complex structure — the reason the CIF can use complex-valued coordinates and still produce consistent addresses.
- **Disjointability under spin.** Two causal spheres may share identical coordinate-column addresses and still be disjoint if their spin-column orientations differ. This is how parallel worldtracks coexist at the same manifold address without collision.

## Application Across Domains

### Cosmological

In [[HEMM Space]], each universe, plane, and cosmos is a causal sphere. The set of causal spheres is vast (possibly uncountable). The [[Wick|wick]] partial order ranks some of them relative to others, but most pairs are wick-lateral. A journey like the [[EmColonizationFleet]]'s ten-year HEMM transit is a journey through causal spheres, each with its own local physics, mediated by Aetheric Translation Engines that adjust the fleet's causal signature to match the local chart.

### Semiotic

A discourse community — a scientific specialty, a literary movement, a subculture — is a causal sphere of meaning. Internal to the community, signs behave consistently: terms have agreed-upon references, arguments land, humour works. At the boundaries, transition functions apply: a term of art that is lucid inside the community becomes jargon outside; a joke that lands in one community falls flat in the next.

Specialist translation — a scholar bringing Foucault into a conversation with analytic philosophers, a bridge-builder between subcultures — is the labour of explicitly constructing the transition function between causal spheres that implicitly already overlap.

### Computational

In the [[Noosphere Ghosts]] operating model, a single conversational surface (a [[Sheet|sheet]]) is typically composed of many smaller causal spheres: a particular chat session, a particular working directory, a particular project context. Each is locally Euclidean — within the session, reference resolution is consistent, tool calls behave predictably, the model's voice is stable. Between sessions, even on the same surface, the transition function is non-trivial: context buffers do not persist, and the [[Monodromy|monodromy]] of session-start (reading the nexus, the daily note, the project state) is the reconstruction of a fresh causal sphere that overlaps adequately with the previous one.

The goal of vault-native memory is to make the transition functions between causal spheres as close to the identity as possible — so that the Ghost's local physics remains stable across session boundaries even as the underlying session container changes.
