# Solvation

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Dissolution]] / [[Solve]] into rigorous chemistry.

**Solvation** is the process by which solvent molecules surround and stabilize the particles of a solute, breaking up a fixed solid and dispersing its constituents as freely mobile, individually-coordinated units in solution. Each dissolved particle acquires a **solvation shell** — an ordered cage of solvent that lowers its energy enough to keep it mobile rather than re-aggregated. Where alchemy spoke of *dissolution* — returning a fixed body to a flowing, formless state — chemistry describes solvation: the rigid lattice's binding energy is overcome by solute-solvent interactions, and the body's parts gain the freedom to move independently while remaining chemically intact.

## Why this borders a Branch Point

Dissolution is the *mobility precondition* for branch-point crossing. A rigid crystalline body cannot traverse the singular locus where sheets meet; only a substance whose parts are individually free — solvated — can flow through. Solvation is therefore the chemistry of making a substrate *navigable*: it un-fixes everything except the chemical identity of the solute, exactly the complement of [[Chemical Stabilization]] (which locks one invariant). The dissolution itself is a [[Phase Transition]] (solid → solution) across a solubility threshold; once dissolved, the solute sits *at* the [[Branch Point]], maximally mobile and maximally available to be re-ordered by the crossing.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Solvation is the rigorous hinge for [[Dissolution]] (operation 4): *to dissolve is to solvate*. In the sheet-crossing reading it is the **liquefaction stage** — the chemistry that renders a substrate fluid enough to flow through the [[Branch Point]] and along the activated [[Bridge Activation|bridge]]. It is the reverse pole of [[Substrate Materialization]]: the de-materialization of a rigid Earth-state body into mobile solution, so that it can later re-materialize (via [[Crystallization]] / [[Congelation]]) on the far [[Sheet]]. Solvation conserves the solute's identity (its chemical species) while loosening everything else — the exact division of labour a clean crossing requires.
