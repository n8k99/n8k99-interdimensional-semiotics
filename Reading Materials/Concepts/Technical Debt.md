# Technical Debt

> Shortcuts taken today accrue interest, paid in slower future development.

**Attribution:** Ward Cunningham, 1992
**Category:** Quality

## Statement

The metaphor is precise: debt can be strategic (borrow now, ship fast, repay later) or ruinous (compound interest eventually exceeds productive capacity). Untracked debt is the dangerous kind — you cannot repay what you do not measure.
