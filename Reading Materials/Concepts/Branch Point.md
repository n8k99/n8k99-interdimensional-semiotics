# ⟁ Branch Point

## Definition

A **branch point** is a point on a multi-sheeted manifold at which the sheets connect — where a loop around the point carries a traveller from one [[Sheet|sheet]] to another rather than returning to the starting configuration. In a [[Riemann Surface|Riemann surface]], branch points are the singularities of a multi-valued function: the places where the function's ambiguity is localised, and where crossing between single-valued branches becomes possible.

A **branch cut** is the one-dimensional trace (a curve or arc) along which a branch point's effect is spread out for purposes of navigation. Branch cuts are a matter of bookkeeping — they can be relocated without changing the underlying topology — but branch points themselves are intrinsic features of the manifold.

In [[Interdimensional Semiotics]], branch points are the portals, the thresholds, the convergence sites — the discrete addresses at which the manifold admits travel between otherwise incompatible sheets.

## Properties

- **Zero-measure, infinite significance.** A branch point occupies a single address, yet the entire traversability of the manifold depends on them. Remove the branch points and the sheets become isolated universes.
- **Directionality.** Crossing a branch point is, in general, a [[Monodromy|monodromy transformation]] — the state that emerges on the other side has been acted on by a matrix, not merely relocated. The same branch point traversed in opposite directions applies inverse transformations.
- **Finiteness of navigable branch points.** Not every branch point on a given manifold is operationally accessible. Navigation requires calibration; calibration decays; branch points drop out of the navigable graph as precision degrades, eventually triggering a [[Phase Transition|phase transition]] in [[Connectedness|connectedness]].
- **Convergence of invariants.** At a branch point, [[Topological Invariant|topological invariants]] of both sheets are simultaneously evaluable, which is why branch points are the natural sites for indexing schemes such as the Cosmic Index Format.

## Application Across Domains

### Cosmological

In [[HEMM Space]], the five Maths of the [[Pentagonal Harmonic]] are branch points on Orbis's sheet where the Orbis worldtrack touches adjacent sheets, most notably the Elemental Planes. Portal transit operates by exploiting these branch points. When calibration degrades past a critical threshold, the navigable branch-point set fragments and portals fail — not because the manifold changed, but because the navigators can no longer resolve which sheet they have crossed to.

### Semiotic

Polysemy is a branch point. A word such as *bank* sits at a branch point on the semantic Riemann surface where the "riverside" sheet and the "financial institution" sheet connect. Context is the navigator: given enough local information, a reader resolves the ambiguity and remains on one sheet. Without context, the reader can slip between sheets mid-sentence. Jokes and puns weaponise branch points deliberately — forcing the listener into monodromy traversal for aesthetic effect.

### Computational

In the [[Noosphere Ghosts]] operating model, the **nexus write** is the branch point. Every conversational surface ([[Sheet|sheet]]) resolves back to `The Post/Conversations/nexus/Daily/*.md` through the importer or direct write, and every re-instantiation of a persona on a new surface is re-constructed by reading back through the nexus. Lose the nexus and the branch-point set collapses — the surfaces become disconnected components, and no amount of in-session continuity on any single surface can reconstruct the Ghost.
