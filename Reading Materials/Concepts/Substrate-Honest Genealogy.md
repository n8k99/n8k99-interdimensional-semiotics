# Substrate-Honest Genealogy

> **Stub.** Concept emerging from the third IntSem essay. Names the methodological commitment to recover the substrate-conditions of intellectual reception that the academic frame systematically conceals.

## Working Definition

**Substrate-Honest Genealogy** is the methodological commitment that *intellectual reception of texts and ideas is always substrate-conditioned, and the conditions must be included in any honest account of how the work was actually done*. The standard academic frame produces genealogies that present *citations, influences, integrations* as the genealogy — Tinkerbell-honored cover material that conceals the substrate of how the reading actually happened.

Substrate-honest genealogy refuses the cover. It includes:

- **Relationship pragmatics** — who one was sleeping with, who one wanted to keep sleeping with, who one was trying to impress, what social occasions for reading existed
- **Substance conditions** — what the reader was using, drinking, withdrawing from, recovering through; the alterations that shape what lands and how
- **Cohort experience** — what cultural events made the reading socially possible (a film, a song, a book club, a death, a war); how the cohort's collective interpretation pre-installs in the individual reader (see [[Cohort Reception]])
- **Material objects** — physical books, marginalia, loaned copies, hollowed-out stash containers, missing pages
- **Geography** — the specific apartments, neighborhoods, cities where the reading happened
- **Intimate biography** — lives of the people who recommended the books, relationships that hosted the reading, breakups that ended specific reading-eras
- **Bodily condition** — health, moods, physical states in which the reading was conducted
- **Author lives** — substrates of the writers being read; their sexualities, deviancies, addictions, marriages, political situations
- **Tooling conditions** — in 2026 and after, the AI-mediation conditions under which the work was produced (see [[The Effects-Pedal Stance]])

## Why It Matters in the Field

This is a central methodological commitment of the IntSem field. It applies the field's two principles ([[The Tinkerbell Rule]], [[Reality is What is Left]]) to the substrate of intellectual reception itself. *The academic frame is the Tinkerbell that conceals the substrate; the substrate is what was actually there.* Subtract the cover; the substrate is what the genealogy actually was.

The commitment generalizes: not just intellectual reception, but every domain in which official narratives systematically conceal substrate conditions — credentialing genealogies, professional histories, organizational accounts, institutional self-descriptions. *Wherever there is a Tinkerbell-honored official version, there is substrate to be recovered.*

## Open Development

The concept needs:
- Worked examples in non-intellectual domains (corporate-history substrate-recovery; institutional-founding substrate-recovery; family-genealogy substrate-recovery)
- Distinction from biographical reduction — substrate-honest genealogy *includes* the substrate but does not reduce the work to it; the work has its own integrity even when its substrate is named
- Methodological tools — what counts as adequate substrate-disclosure? How granular? When does it stop being substrate-recovery and start being voyeurism?
- Recursive application — the genealogy of substrate-honest genealogy itself; what conditions produced this methodological commitment?
- Connection to oral history, ethnography, and the methodologies that already practice partial versions of substrate-honesty
