# Dunbar's Number

> Humans can maintain roughly 150 stable social relationships.

**Attribution:** Robin Dunbar, 1992
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

Beyond that threshold, social cohesion requires structure — hierarchy, ritual, formal process — because cognition alone can no longer track who is who. Engineering organizations feel this wall at the point where informal communication quietly stops scaling.
