# Premature Optimization

> Premature optimization is the root of all evil.

**Attribution:** Donald Knuth, 1974
**Category:** Planning

## Statement

Knuth's full line is: "We should forget about small efficiencies, say about 97% of the time: premature optimization is the root of all evil." The point is not that optimization is bad — it's that optimizing before you know where the cost actually lives produces complexity without performance.
