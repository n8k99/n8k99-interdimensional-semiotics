# Conway's Law

> Organizations design systems that mirror their own communication structure.

**Attribution:** Melvin Conway, 1968
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

Any software system inevitably encodes the social graph of the team that produced it. Module boundaries follow team boundaries; interfaces follow org-chart seams. You cannot ship an architecture whose shape contradicts how the humans who build it actually talk to each other.
