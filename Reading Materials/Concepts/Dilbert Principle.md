# Dilbert Principle

> Companies tend to promote their least competent employees to management, to minimize the damage they can do.

**Attribution:** Scott Adams, 1995
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

A cynical inversion of the Peter Principle: rather than promotion until incompetence, promotion *because of* incompetence — management as a quarantine for people who would do more harm in production roles.
