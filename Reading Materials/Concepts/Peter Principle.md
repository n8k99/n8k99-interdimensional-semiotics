# Peter Principle

> In a hierarchy, every employee tends to rise to their level of incompetence.

**Attribution:** Laurence J. Peter, 1969
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

Success at one level is rewarded with promotion, and promotion continues until the employee reaches a role they cannot perform. The system then stops promoting them — leaving every position occupied by someone who cannot do it well.
