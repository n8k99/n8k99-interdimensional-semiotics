# Amdahl's Law

> The speedup of a program using multiple processors is limited by the fraction of the program that cannot be parallelized.

**Attribution:** Gene Amdahl, 1967
**Category:** Scale

## Statement

If 10% of a workload is serial, no amount of parallelism will speed the whole workload by more than 10×. The serial remainder dominates asymptotically. Parallelization gains have a ceiling set by the non-parallel slice.
