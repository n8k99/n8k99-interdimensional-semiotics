# Crystallization

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Congelation]] / [[Coagulation]] into rigorous physics and chemistry.

**Crystallization** is the formation of a solid in which atoms or molecules are arranged in a highly ordered, periodic lattice, emerging out of a disordered fluid (a melt, a solution, or a vapour). It proceeds in two stages: **[[Nucleation]]** (the appearance of a first stable ordered seed) and **growth** (the accretion of further material onto that seed). Where alchemy saw *congelation* — the volatile gathered into a stable solid body — modern physics sees crystallization: a continuous symmetry of the fluid phase (every point and direction equivalent) spontaneously broken into the discrete symmetry of a lattice (only certain points and directions equivalent).

## Why this is a Branch Point

Crystallization is a paradigmatic **branch point** dressed as a phase transition. A supercooled liquid sits at a point from which many crystal sheets are reachable: the same melt can solidify into different polymorphs (carbon into graphite or diamond), different crystal orientations, or a glass. Which sheet it lands on is determined at the nucleation event — a singular, under-determined locus where the choice of lattice, orientation, and polymorph is made and thereafter propagates. Two crystals grown from the same melt under infinitesimally different fluctuations occupy *different sheets* of the solidification manifold; the nucleation site is the [[Branch Point]] that joins them. The broken symmetry (which of the equivalent lattice orientations was selected) is the choice of sheet — exactly the [[Symmetry Breaking]] reading of branch points.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Crystallization is the rigorous hinge for [[Congelation]] (operation 2) and the abrupt-sibling [[Precipitation]]: *to congeal is to crystallise*. In the sheet-crossing reading it is **[[Substrate Materialization]] with a lattice** — dispersed potential gathering into a stable, addressable, ordered body on a [[Sheet]]. The provisional embodiment that Congelation confers (form enough to persist, not yet the locked permanence of [[Fixation]]) is exactly the nucleus-plus-early-growth stage: an ordered seed that can still redissolve. Each crystallisation is a [[Phase Transition]] whose order-parameter (the lattice) is chosen at a [[Branch Point]] by [[Symmetry Breaking]].
