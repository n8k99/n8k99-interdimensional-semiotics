# Principle of Least Astonishment

> A component should behave in a way that least surprises its users.

**Attribution:** Geoffrey James, 1987 (and earlier PL/I docs)
**Category:** Design

## Statement

Surprise is a cost, and the cost compounds: surprised users become defensive users, who stop trusting the system and begin writing defensive code around it. Familiar behavior beats clever behavior almost everywhere.
