# Gustafson's Law

> If the problem size scales with available processors, parallel speedup can continue to grow.

**Attribution:** John L. Gustafson, 1988
**Category:** Scale

## Statement

The counterpoint to Amdahl: in practice, more compute is usually used to solve *bigger* problems, not the *same* problem faster. Under that regime the serial bottleneck matters less and linear speedup is achievable.
