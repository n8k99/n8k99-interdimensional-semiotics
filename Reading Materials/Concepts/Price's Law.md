# Price's Law

> Half the work in any group is done by the square root of the participants.

**Attribution:** Derek J. de Solla Price
**Category:** Teams

## Statement

In a team of 100, ~10 people do half the substantive work. Productivity distributes by a power law, not uniformly. This is descriptive, not prescriptive — but it reliably shows up in commit graphs, citation counts, and bug fixes.
