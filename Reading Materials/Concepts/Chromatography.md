# Chromatography

> Minted [[2026-06-19]] as the **modern-scientific middle rung** translating the alchemical operation [[Separation]] (and the [[Filtration]] process) into rigorous chemistry.

**Chromatography** separates the components of a mixture by their differential affinity between two phases: a **stationary phase** (a fixed medium) and a **mobile phase** (a fluid carrying the mixture past it). Each component partitions differently — some bind the stationary phase strongly and lag, others stay mobile and race ahead — so a single mixed band fans out into distinct, separated bands. It is the rigorous, repeatable generalisation of every classical separation: distillation by volatility, filtration by size, chromatography by affinity. Where alchemy spoke of *separation* — parting the subtle from the gross — chemistry gives the precise mechanism: differential partition across a phase boundary resolves a multi-component substance into single components.

## Why this resolves a Branch Point

Chromatography *operationalizes the defining feature of a branch point*: that a substance can be **multi-valued**, carrying components that belong to distinct sheets. The mixed band sits at the [[Branch Point]] as a superposition; the column is the apparatus that resolves the multi-valence — each component follows a different continuation (a different migration rate) and emerges onto its own [[Sheet]]. The single→many fan-out is the column-scale image of [[Symmetry Breaking]]: a degenerate mixed state (all components co-located) differentiates into distinct separated states, each component sorted to where it belongs. Chromatography is the analytic instrument that *reads off* the sheet-membership of each part.

## Significance for Interdimensional Semiotics

Chromatography is the rigorous hinge for [[Separation]] (operation 8) and the mechanical [[Filtration]] process: *to separate is to chromatograph*. In the sheet-crossing reading it is the **branch-point-resolution stage** — the operation that parts a single prima materia along the seams where its components belong to different [[Sheet|sheets]], sending each to its proper sheet by differential passage. Where the carrying operations move a substance *across* a boundary, chromatography is the recognition that a single substance *straddles* a branch point and must be divided along it — the analytic moment that precedes any clean re-combination ([[Conjunction]]).
