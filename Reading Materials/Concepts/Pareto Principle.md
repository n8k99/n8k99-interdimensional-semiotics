# Pareto Principle

> Roughly 80% of effects come from 20% of causes.

**Attribution:** Vilfredo Pareto, 1906
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

Found empirically in wealth distribution, bug sources, feature usage, customer revenue, and code hot paths. Operationalized as: find the 20% that matters, spend your attention there, and be suspicious of effort on the other 80%.
