# The Effects-Pedal Stance

> **Stub.** Methodological commitment named in the third IntSem essay. Describes the author's stance toward AI as one of *systematic-rigorous instrumental play* — the same stance applied to any new tool — neither evangelical adoption nor moralized refusal.

## Working Definition

**The Effects-Pedal Stance** is the methodological position of approaching AI (or any new tool with contested status) the way a working musician approaches a new effects pedal: *find the limitations, find the use cases, find the failure modes, characterize the boundaries, integrate what works, set aside what doesn't, document the experience honestly*. The framing is technical-instrumental, calibrated to the actual operational properties of the tool, not pre-installed by the dominant discourses about what the tool should be.

The stance has two characteristic refusals:

1. **Refusal of uncritical adoption** — *the effects pedal is just a new musical tool, no different from any other; use it freely; ignore the substrate.* This is the evangelist's consoling position. The Effects-Pedal Stance does not adopt this position because it requires ignoring substrate conditions (extractive infrastructure, uncompensated training data, environmental externalities, labor exploitation) that are real.

2. **Refusal of moralistic rejection** — *the effects pedal is structurally compromised; its lack of innocence makes its use indefensible; refuse on principle.* This is the moralist's consoling position. The Effects-Pedal Stance does not adopt this position because it requires pretending to a clean-hands position that is itself a Tinkerbell-honored simulacrum (no contemporary tool has clean hands; all are implicated; refusal of one implicated tool while continuing to use other implicated tools is a moral performance, not a structural commitment).

What remains, after both consolations are refused, is **operating inside the conditions while seeing them**. *Use the tool. See through the tool. Refuse the consolations the discourse provides for both extreme positions. Stay in the moral grey zone honestly.*

## Why It Matters in the Field

The Effects-Pedal Stance is the IntSem-honest stance toward implicated tools — and most contemporary tools are implicated. The alternative stances (uncritical adoption, moralistic refusal) both require Tinkerbell-honoring of conditions that are not actually clean: the evangelist Tinkerbells the technology's costs out of view; the moralist Tinkerbells the cleanness of refusal-positions that are themselves implicated.

The stance generalizes beyond AI. It applies to any tool whose use requires honest acknowledgment of its substrate conditions: smartphones, social media, animal-tested medications, fossil-fuel-powered devices, products of exploited labor, content from deceased problematic authors, services built on compromised infrastructure. *The Effects-Pedal Stance is what you do when you cannot have clean hands but also cannot stop operating in the world.*

## Connection to Other IntSem Methodology

- **Aligned with the Foucauldian inheritance** (third IntSem essay): operate inside the dynamics one is anatomizing; refuse the consoling outside-the-system position
- **Aligned with the Baudrillardian rejection of consolation** — there is no outside; the red-pill fantasy is itself a fourth-order simulacrum; the only honest position is inside-while-seeing-through
- **Aligned with [[Counterinduction]]** (Feyerabend): refuse the methodological orthodoxy the discourse provides; cultivate stances incompatible with the dominant moralizations
- **Aligned with Chaotic Neutral alignment** as Nathan articulated it earlier — operate from interest and impulse, do not hold strong ethical positions, document the contradictions on the table
