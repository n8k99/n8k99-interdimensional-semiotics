# Dunning-Kruger Effect

> People with limited knowledge or competence in a domain tend to overestimate their ability in that domain.

**Attribution:** Dunning & Kruger, 1999
**Category:** Decisions

## Statement

The metacognitive skills needed to recognize incompetence are the same skills one lacks when incompetent. The inverse — imposter syndrome in experts — is often the same effect from the other side.
