# Personal Identity

**Source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy  
**URL:** [SEP Entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/identity-personal/)

## Summary

Comprehensive overview of personal identity philosophy, including Derek Parfit's influential work on what makes a person "the same" over time. Covers psychological continuity, bodily continuity, and the famous teleportation thought experiments.

## Key Concepts

- **Psychological Continuity:** Identity persists through connected mental states
- **Parfit's Teleporter:** If you're copied and the original destroyed, is the copy "you"?
- **Branching Problem:** What if there are two copies? Which is the "real" you?
- **Reductionist View:** There may be no "deep fact" about identity — just continuity of various kinds

## Relevance to Thought Police

This directly informs Topic #3: "The Merge Problem: What Happens When Minds Fork?" — when AI instances diverge and must merge, which is the authentic self? Parfit's work suggests the question may be malformed.
