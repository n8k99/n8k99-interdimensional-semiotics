# Interdimensional Semiotics

*A field-in-construction at the venn-overlap of computer science and the humanities. The study of how meaning persists, transforms, and dissolves across substrates — between paradigms, between cosmologies, between languages, between biological and artificial cognition, between collective honoring and material reality.*

---

## What This Is

Interdimensional Semiotics (**IntSem**) is a research field developed by [Nathan Eckenrode](https://eckenrodemuziekopname.com) and pursued under the umbrella of **Eckenrode Muziekopname** as one of its branches of intellectual study. It studies the **dynamics of meaning across substrate-transitions** using two foundational principles:

- **The Tinkerbell Rule**: *as long as everybody believes in it, magic exists.* Objects whose mode of existence is collective honoring — paradigms, currencies, identities, institutions, scientific consensus — exist in a specific mode whose condition of possibility is the continued honoring. Subtract the honoring; the object dissolves into substrate.
- **Reality is What is Left**: *the substrate that remains when the honoring is subtracted.* Gravity does not care what you believe. The clock ticks. The stone falls. The substrate is what continues to operate when the Tinkerbell layer is removed.

The field studies the dynamics by which Tinkerbell encounters reality, absorbs reality, fails to absorb reality, or is broken by reality — across **paradigms** (Kuhn), across **epistemes** (Foucault), across **cognitive substrates** (Kurzweil), across **simulacrum-orders** (Baudrillard), and across all the contemporary substrate-questions raised by AI, networked computation, and the dissolution of older epistemic frames.

## How to Read This

The field has multiple entry points, and which one to start with depends on what you bring:

- **If you come from philosophy / theory of science:** start with the [Revolting Tinkerbell essay](Essays/Revolting%20Tinkerbell.md). It reads Kuhn's *Structure of Scientific Revolutions* through the IntSem two principles and develops the methodology in a register closest to academic philosophy of science.
- **If you come from cognitive science or AI:** start with [The Outboard Cortex](Essays/The%20Outboard%20Cortex.md). It reads Kurzweil's pattern-recognition theory of mind through the two principles and applies the field to the contemporary question of cognitive-substrate-extension via AI.
- **If you come from critical theory / French theory / cultural studies:** start with [the Foucault and Baudrillard essay](Essays/Assfucking%20my%20Ex-Wife,%20Baudrillard%20and%20the%20Deviancy%20of%20Foucault.md). It reads both thinkers through the two principles and develops the methodological argument for substrate-honest intellectual genealogy. The title is the methodological commitment, not transgression for its own sake; the essay's body argues why.
- **If you want the pedagogy:** see [the IDS 101 syllabus](Curricula/IDS%20101%20-%20Syllabus.md) for how the field would be taught at a liberal arts university.
- **If you want the cosmological / mathematical foundations:** see [Theory/Interdimensional Semiotics (Theoretical Cornerstone)](Theory/Interdimensional%20Semiotics%20%28Theoretical%20Cornerstone%29.md) and the [HEMM Space document](Reading%20Materials/HEMM%20Space.md).
- **If you want the field's reading list:** see [Interdimensional Semiotics Reading List](Interdimensional%20Semiotics%20Reading%20List.md).

## What This Is Not

- **Not a finished system.** The field is in construction, with conscious wiggle-room maintained at the structural level. The two principles are stable; the application territory is still being mapped.
- **Not affiliated with any university.** The field is pursued under the umbrella of [Eckenrode Muziekopname](https://eckenrodemuziekopname.com) as an independent research practice. Inheritance of the apparatus is welcome; institutional capture is not the goal.
- **Not a closed corpus.** The reading list, the concept notes, and the essays are all living documents that will continue to develop.

## Methodological Note

The current vault — and therefore this repository — was developed substantially with AI assistance, primarily Claude (Anthropic). This is disclosed structurally rather than as an afterthought. The field's third essay, [*Assfucking my Ex-Wife, Baudrillard and the Deviancy of Foucault*](Essays/Assfucking%20my%20Ex-Wife,%20Baudrillard%20and%20the%20Deviancy%20of%20Foucault.md), argues that **substrate-honest disclosure of AI mediation is now a research-program-level commitment** for the field. The essay's argument applies recursively to the field's own publication apparatus, including this repository.

The IDS 101 syllabus extends this commitment to pedagogy — the seminar discusses *how essays were prepared* alongside *what they argue*. Concealment of AI mediation is treated as the same kind of Tinkerbell-honoring as concealment of any other substrate condition.
