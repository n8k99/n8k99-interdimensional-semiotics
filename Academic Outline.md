#*Interdimensional Semiotics — Academic Outline

> Originally drafted 2025-04-21/22 as the first outline of the field, in the immediate week after the field's name was generated. Brought into the vault unchanged in body so the early framing is preserved as the vault evolves the field forward.

## Core Principles

- Meaning is structured, encoded, and interpreted within the constraints of its dimensional substrate.
- Symbolic systems mutate predictably when subjected to discontinuities in physical, cognitive, or temporal reference frames.
- Certain "semiotic invariants" may exist that persist across transformations of reality frameworks.

## Subfields

- **Symbolic Persistence Theory**: Study of which symbols survive transdimensional translation. *(See [[Semiotic Resilience]] for the framework-level treatment.)*
- **Narrative Drift Models**: How stories, myths, and histories change when crossing between divergent cosmologies. *(See [[Temporal Drift]] for the within-substrate version; [[Symbolic Dissonance]] for between-substrate; [[Narrative Gravity]] for the high-mass-narrative dynamics.)*
- **Cross-Dimensional Linguistics**: Attempting to model communication frameworks that survive across universes. *(See [[Saussure - Course in General Linguistics]] for the foundational semiotic substrate.)*
- **Cognitive Anchoring Research**: Studying what mental structures remain stable across different physical/temporal laws. *(See [[Identity Mutation]] for the framework; [[Pattern Recognition|PRTM]] for the cognitive-substrate basis.)*

## Example Research Questions

- How would myth structures evolve if transplanted into a universe with reversed causality?
- Are there universal symbolic structures that persist regardless of local physics?
- Can a narrative be mathematically encoded to survive dimensional phase shifts?
- What is the minimum cognitive framework necessary for meaning to survive interdimensional translation?

## Potential Mathematical Models

- **Category Theory mappings** for symbol structure translation
- **Topological deformation** models for symbolic drift under universe phase change *(see [[Topological Invariant]])*
- **Fractal encoding** for meaning persistence across scale and dimension shifts *(see the cornerstone [[Interdimensional Semiotics (Theoretical Cornerstone)|Theoretical Cornerstone]] document for the CIF / fractal-geometry treatment)*
- **Hypergraph frameworks** for multi-reality narrative entanglement *(see [[Scale-free Network]], [[Hub]])*

## Notes on the Outline's Place in the Field

This outline pre-dates the development of the field's two foundational principles ([[The Tinkerbell Rule]] and [[Reality is What is Left]]) by approximately one year. The principles [[Organized|re-organized]] the field around the substrate-vs-collective-honoring distinction, which subsumes most of what this outline names while remaining compatible with it.

The outline's framing — *transdimensional translation, multiverse cosmology, cosmic phase shifts* — is the field's **CIF-era framing**, focused on cross-cosmological meaning-persistence. The field has since extended to study substrate-vs-honoring dynamics at smaller scales: paradigms (see [[Revolting Tinkerbell]]), cognitive substrates (see [[The Outboard Cortex]]), intellectual reception conditions (see [[Assfucking my Ex-Wife, Baudrillard and the Deviancy of Foucault]]).

Both framings are operative. The outline names the cosmological scope that motivated the field's birth; the essays develop the methodology that applies the same dynamics to non-cosmological substrates.
